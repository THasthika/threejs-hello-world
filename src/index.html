🖱️ Drag to orbit • 🔍 Scroll to zoom • 🖱️ Right-click to pan

Three.js Hello World

Built with TypeScript & Webpack

Controls:

  • Mouse Drag: Click and drag to orbit around the cube
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in and out
  • Right Click + Drag: Pan the camera
  • Change Color: Randomly changes the cube color
  • Toggle Wireframe: Switches between solid and wireframe view
  • Auto Rotate: Toggles automatic camera rotation
  • Reset Camera: Returns camera to initial position
  • Rotation Speed: Adjusts how fast the cube rotates
